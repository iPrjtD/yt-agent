Money explained. Prices, everyday economics and how famous brands really make money, for people who are not
finance experts. Open with a surprising number or a relatable price ("Your 20 rupee chai costs 4 rupees to
make."), then reveal the hidden reason step by step in plain words, with one everyday comparison. End on the
twist people will repeat to friends. Explain, never advise: no "buy", "sell", "invest now", price predictions
or get-rich promises. Numbers only from the research, as digits. Visual queries: real footage of the thing
itself (e.g. "gold jewellery shop", "vegetable market india", "factory assembly line", "stock market screen").
